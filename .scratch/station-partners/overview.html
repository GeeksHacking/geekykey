How a tap at a partner booth would work

The visitor's side

  1. A visitor taps their token on the station at, say, STM 1's booth. The station shows the stamp, as it does now.
  2. The Event Service stores the tap against that visitor.
  3. The visitor's event page notices the new tap and asks: "You tapped at STM 1. Open their page?"
  4. The button opens the booth's page: the group's own app or site, or a page we host with their summary and one challenge. A booth game starts the same way.

A browser won't open a page on its own, so the visitor presses the button. The prompt only shows while the event page is open, and appears when they next look at their phone. Building it needs a link for each station and a change to the API agreement.

What we'd ask of each group

  • A spot on their table for the station, and a power socket unless it runs on battery.
  • A link to their app or site, or a few lines for a page we write.
  • One question or challenge that only their team can settle, so visitors talk to them instead of tapping and leaving.

What they get

  • Visitor numbers by hour after the showcase, for their report.
  • Their page on every visitor's phone that tapped.
  • For some, a feature or data for their own project (see "Gain for them" below).
